Our Story

Riddhi Siddhi Fashion is a family-run manufacturer and wholesaler of premium kids boys shirts based in Jalore, Rajasthan. Our brand Jay&Joy was born out of a simple idea: kids deserve comfortable, stylish, and durable clothing — at honest prices.

Every Jay&Joy shirt is carefully designed with soft, breathable fabrics, vibrant prints, and strong stitching that survives playground adventures. From casual everyday shirts to formal party wear, we make clothing that kids love and parents trust.
