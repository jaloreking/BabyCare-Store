Shipping Information

At Kids Shirt Studio, we strive to deliver your orders quickly and securely. Here's everything you need to know about our shipping process.

Delivery Timeline

  • Standard Delivery: 3-5 business days
  • Express Delivery: 1-2 business days (available at checkout)
  • Same Day Delivery: Available in select cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore)

Delivery times may vary based on location and order volume.

Shipping Charges

  • Standard Shipping: ₹49 for orders below ₹999
  • Free Shipping: On all orders above ₹999
  • Express Shipping: ₹99 (flat rate)
  • Same Day Delivery: ₹199 (select cities only)

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can track your order status using our tracking page or the courier partner's website.

Delivery Areas

We deliver to all pin codes across India. For international shipping inquiries, please contact our customer support team.

Shipping Partners

We partner with trusted courier services including:

  • Blue Dart
  • Delhivery
  • DTDC
  • India Post (for remote areas)

Important Notes

  • Orders placed after 6 PM will be processed the next business day
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by natural disasters or unforeseen circumstances
  • Please ensure your shipping address is correct to avoid delivery delays
