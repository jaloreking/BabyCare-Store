Shipping & Cash on Delivery

Everything you need to know about delivery and payment.

🚚 Shipping

We ship pan-India from our facility in Jalore, Rajasthan. Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of confirmation on WhatsApp.

  • ✅ Delivery in 3–7 business days (depending on location)
  • ✅ Free shipping on prepaid orders above ₹999
  • ✅ COD available across most pin codes

💵 Cash on Delivery

Pay only when your shirts arrive at your doorstep. No advance payment needed. COD is available on all standard orders.

🔄 Returns & Exchange

If you receive a damaged or wrong item, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll arrange an exchange or refund.

📦 Bulk / Wholesale

For wholesale orders (10+ pieces), special shipping terms and discounts apply. Contact us on WhatsApp for a quote.
